THE WORLD / SECOND LIVES

如果别人的一生，
成为了你的记忆。

What if another life
became part of your memory?

三位当代女性，通过名为“复制人生”的科幻装置，体验陈圆圆、蔡文姬与萧皇后的人生。现代身份与历史经验相互碰撞：过去成为她们的记忆，也改变她们当下的关系与选择。

Three contemporary women experience the lives of Chen Yuanyuan, Cai Wenji and Empress Xiao through a fictional device that recreates a life. Modern identities and historical experience collide: the past becomes part of their memory and reshapes their relationships and choices.

她们带着各自的欲望、立场与盲点，进入同一个世界。历史提供局面，人物必须自己面对后果。

Each enters the same world with her own desires, convictions and blind spots. History provides the circumstances; the characters must face the consequences.

叙事与自我STORY & SELF

陈眠MianMIAN / 原创角色ORIGINAL CHARACTER

小说家与模特，克制而敏锐。她想掌握被观看的方式；陈圆圆的人生，却让她重新追问故事由谁书写。

A novelist and model, reserved and perceptive. She wants control over how she is seen. Chen Yuanyuan’s life makes her question who gets to write the story.

她的第二人生：陈圆圆Her second life: Chen Yuanyuan

当名声、欲望与选择纠缠在一起，掌握叙事能否真的改变处境？

When reputation, desire and choice become entangled, can control of a story change a life?

科学与归属SCIENCE & BELONGING

蔡医生SylviaSYLVIA / 原创角色ORIGINAL CHARACTER

温暖的发明者，珍视爱人与家庭。她能解释许多机制，却仍要面对蔡文姬的人生带来的情感与归属。

A warm inventor who values her partner and family. She can explain many mechanisms, yet must face the emotions and belonging carried by Cai Wenji’s life.

她的第二人生：蔡文姬Her second life: Cai Wenji

理解一个人的感受，和真正承受那段人生，会是同一件事吗？

Is understanding a person’s feelings the same as living with the weight of their experience?

资源与信任RESOURCES & TRUST

萧VesperVESPER / 原创角色ORIGINAL CHARACTER

擅长数学与金融，日常松弛，面对利益时敏锐果断。萧皇后的经历，让资源、失控与信任成为她无法回避的命题。

Skilled in mathematics and finance, relaxed in daily life and decisive when interests are at stake. Empress Xiao’s experience confronts her with resources, lost control and trust.

她的第二人生：萧皇后Her second life: Empress Xiao

当局势无法再被算清，她愿意把命运的一部分交给谁？

When a situation can no longer be calculated, who will she trust with part of her fate?
