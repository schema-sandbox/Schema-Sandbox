THE QUESTION

谁在定义
你的美？

Who gets to define
your beauty?

看似关于外表，
其实关乎如何判断、如何选择。

It begins with appearance.
It reaches into judgment and choice.

《美，不可言》从审美、身体与情绪出发，讨论外部标准、事实依据、选择代价，以及自己真正的愿望。

Beauty, Beyond Words begins with aesthetics, the body and emotion, and opens a conversation about external standards, evidence, consequences and what you want for yourself.

MR.TENG 提出观点，一位原创角色追问、质疑与交锋。思想在对话中被检验，判断的位置留给观众。

MR.TENG offers a perspective. An original character asks, questions and challenges. Ideas are tested in dialogue, with space left for the audience's judgment.

01 / BEAUTY

美，如何成为标准？

How does beauty
become a standard?

从比例、对称与审美经验出发，追问流行标准的来源与边界。

From proportion and symmetry to aesthetic experience: where do familiar standards come from, and where do they stop?

02 / EMOTION

情绪，怎样参与判断？

How does emotion
shape judgment?

看见身体感受和情绪信号，也把依据、关系与现实代价放进讨论。

Recognizing bodily experience and emotional signals while considering evidence, relationships and real consequences.

03 / AGENCY

选择，最后属于谁？

Whose choice
is it, in the end?

分辨自己的愿望与他人的期待，在复杂处境中保留行动的主导权。

Distinguishing your wishes from other people's expectations, and keeping agency in complex situations.

THE AUTHOR & THE CONVERSATION

一场有来有往
的思考。

A conversation
that pushes back.

作者提出判断。
角色把判断放回真实处境。

The author offers a judgment.
The character brings it back to lived experience.

MR.TENG 为真实作者，以本人的数字形象与声线表达；对话角色属于原创虚构人物。MR.TENG is a real author, expressed through his own digital likeness and voice. The conversation partner is an original fictional character.

01

真实作者，持续表达

A real author, a continuing perspective

由 MR.TENG 的思想、知识与经历出发，连接节目、书籍、课程及专题作品。

Rooted in MR.TENG's ideas, knowledge and experience, connecting the series with books, courses and thematic work.

02

短篇对话，保留追问

Short dialogues, room for challenge

以观点、追问与回应展开，让视频与音频都能独立成立。

Built around a perspective, a challenge and a response, with video and audio each able to stand on their own.

03

从观看，进入自己的判断

From watching to thinking for yourself

通过议题、例子与知识工具，让讨论与观众自身的生活选择发生关系。

Topics, examples and knowledge tools connect the discussion to the audience's own decisions.
